We believe that a world is possible where the world organisation would be such that there  would be the highest chance that each sentient being would naturally feel as well as possible over time (in all aspects of wellbeing).

While it is likely possible, many problems are blocking its reach such as the weight of legacy on organisations and people's believes and habits, and the complexity of the world and the unclear definition of what is good or right, all of it into a world where resources are unevenly distributed and where uncertainties for the future is real (diseases, disasters, ..)

Given and despite limited resources, HappyBein actually aims at providing the highest leverage support to bring us as close and fast to our vision.
