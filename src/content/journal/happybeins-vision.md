We believe a world is possible where things are organized such that every sentient being has the highest chance of naturally feeling as well as possible over time, across all aspects of wellbeing.

Reaching it is blocked by real problems: the weight of legacy in our organizations, beliefs, and habits; the sheer complexity of the world and the unclear definition of what's good or right; and a world where resources are unevenly distributed and real uncertainties — disease, disaster — remain part of the picture.

Given our limited resources, HappyBein aims to provide the highest-leverage support toward that vision, as close and as fast as we realistically can.
