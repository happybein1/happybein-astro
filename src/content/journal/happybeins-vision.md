**The Ultimate Goal:** We believe a world is possible where things are organized such that every sentient being has the highest chance of naturally feeling as well as possible over time, across all aspects of wellbeing.

**The Main Challenges:** Reaching it is blocked by real problems: the weight of legacy in our organizations, beliefs, and habits; the sheer complexity of the world and the unclear definition of what's good or right; and a world where resources are unevenly distributed and real uncertainties — disease, disaster — remain part of the picture.

**Our Main Goal:** Given our limited resources, HappyBein aims to provide the highest-leverage support toward that vision, as close and as fast as we realistically can.

**How we plan on Achieving it:** 

* We learned a lot about the world, but especially what makes sentient beings feel well or bad, how decision making is efficiently made, but also the current state of the world and investigating possible solutions to some big challenges
* Still looking to make an impact, we are building tools to helps us make a higher impact better decisions, plans, and habit changes.
* We documented our research and findings so that it is transparent and can be used by anyone
* We tried to consider all these aspects from an objective perspective, hence almost starting from a blank page.
