This entry looks back at what has been built so far, to help decide what comes next.

## Where it started

HappyBein began in 2021 with a simple observation: well-being and good decisions depend on
things most people are never taught, from how our bodies and minds react to stress, to how
we decide and how we work with others. We set up a non-profit foundation, HappyBein
Stichting, to share that knowledge in plain language.

The first steps were a website, explainer videos for YouTube, and protecting the HappyBein
brand. We also tried branded t-shirts and mugs, to see whether the brand could help fund
the work.

## Listening, then restructuring

We presented the idea to coaches and other professionals. The reactions were mixed: many
were keen to connect, but the value of HappyBein wasn't always clear to them. One piece of
feedback stood out: a tool that helps someone set goals would be genuinely useful.

That told us we weren't there yet. We paused the branding work and restructured the
project. The educational content moved to a new home, YeahVibe, and was improved along the
way.

## Adding context: YeahSight and YeahDev

Good decisions often need a high-level understanding of where society is and where it's
heading. That's why we added **YeahSight**, which looks at how society works, the
challenges it faces, and how they might be solved. We also started **YeahDev**, to share
the development know-how we were picking up.

## From videos to tools

We tried marketing the content through short videos on social platforms. It took a lot of
time and the results were disappointing, but it taught us useful skills in AI tools and
modern marketing.

It also led to a key insight: the content creates far more value when it's built into
tools people use every day. So we started building simple web apps, now gathered at
**yeahvibe.com**. The foundation content became **YeahCore**, at yeahcore.happybein.com.

## IntentFlow and ShopTools

Two apps have had most of our attention since:

- **IntentFlow**: habits, plans and an agenda, all in one place.
- **ShopTools**: groceries, household stock, stores and a budget. It started as "Shop" and
  grew enough beyond groceries to earn a new name.

Both are free and store data on your device by default, with optional cloud sync for
people who switch devices. A small group of early users now uses them every day, and their
feedback has shaped most of what we've built since. Both are also packaged as Android apps:
ShopTools is partway through its Google Play submission, and IntentFlow is next.
